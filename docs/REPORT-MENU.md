# Avante Magnet Health Reports — Menu

Pick what you'd like to receive, how much it should cover, and how often.
Each item lists what it shows, who it's for, and how it arrives.

---

## The reports

### 1. Magnet Health Brief
**One page per MRI.** The quickest way to see how a single magnet is doing.

- **What's on it:**
  - Five headline numbers: compressor, helium level, helium pressure, and, depending on the system, coldhead, cabinet or room temperature.
  - Two charts: pressure (or shield temperature) and helium over the period, with alert limits drawn in.
  - A short plain-English story of what happened.
  - Current readings and rates.
- **Best for:** a quick check on one system, or sharing with a site contact.
- **Arrives as:** a PDF attachment. Several briefs arrive together in one email; more than four come as a single zip file.

### 2. Magnet Health Summary: one customer
**Every magnet for one customer in one document.** It can also be narrowed to particular sites or systems.

- **What's on it:**
  - A cover that says how many systems need attention and which ones are urgent.
  - The list of systems that need a look, worst first.
  - A table for each manufacturer with every system's current readings.
  - Environmental (EDU) readings.
  - The site groups and who in your organization has access.
- **Best for:** weekly or monthly oversight across a whole customer.
- **Arrives as:** one email with the PDF attached. The email itself lists every system and its status, so you can scan it without opening the attachment.

### 3. Magnet Health Summary: full fleet *(Avante internal)*
The same document as #2, covering every monitored magnet across all customers.

- **Best for:** Avante's daily or weekly review of the whole fleet.
- **Arrives as:** one email with the PDF attached.

### 4. Personal digest
**Your own summary, limited to the systems you have access to.** If you have access to several customers, you get one document for each, all in a single email.

- **Best for:** individual users who want a regular update without asking for one.
- **Arrives as:** one email. A single document is attached as a PDF; several arrive as one zip file.

### 5. System Activity *(add-on to a Summary)*
**An interactive page for every system that needs a closer look.** Open it in any web browser; it works offline.

- **What's on it:**
  - A dashboard of the flagged systems. Click one to see its timeline: readings, alerts triggered, and the notifications sent and to whom.
  - When a compressor stopped, a card for each stop explaining whether other data backs it up.
- **Two versions:**
  - **Customer copy:** shows only your organization's people.
  - **Internal copy** *(Avante only)*: also includes Avante staff.
- **Arrives as:** a zip file attached to the Summary email. Very large sets are split into follow-up emails.

### 6. Anomaly Report *(on request)*
**A deep dive into one incident on one system.** It covers a specific window, such as the two days around a compressor stop.

- **What's on it:** a minute-by-minute timeline of readings, alerts and notifications, what likely happened, and the steps being taken.
- **Two versions:** customer and internal.
- **Arrives as:** a file shared by your Avante contact. It is not sent automatically.

### 7. Alert-style email reports
**Short table emails driven by a threshold you set.** Examples: helium level below a set value, helium pressure, systems offline, and open issues.

- **Best for:** a simple checklist-style email on a regular schedule.
- **Arrives as:** a plain email with a table. Nothing is sent when there's nothing to report.

### Internal tools *(Avante only)*
- **GE CT Insights:** one interactive page covering the GE CT fleet, built on request.

## Choose your options

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Which systems** | One MRI, a list of MRIs, one or more sites, or everything for a customer |
| **How far back** | 7 days, 30 days *(standard)*, 90 days, 6 months, or any exact dates and times (e.g. "Oct 9, 2 pm to Oct 11, 6 pm") |
| **How often** | One time, or on a schedule: any day of the week, any half-hour, Eastern time |
| **Who receives it** | Any list of email addresses, plus CC |
| **Add-ons** | System Activity pages (Summary reports only) |

## What you'll always see vs. what appears only when it applies

**Always included**
- The period covered, stated on the cover and in the email.
- The current status of every system included.
- The alert limits that each reading is compared against.

**Only when it applies**

| You'll see… | When… |
|---|---|
| **Needs attention** / **Urgent** | A compressor stopped, pressure is rising, or a reading went over its limit. "Urgent" means it is still a problem now. |
| **"May not be off"** or **"Unconfirmed"** after a compressor stop | The vibration sensor reported the compressor off, but the scanner and the magnet's temperature don't fully agree. We still report the stop. The note tells you how sure we are. |
| **Sensor suspect** / **No signal** banner | The readings look like a monitoring fault rather than a magnet problem. Values are shown but not judged. |
| **Quench** | A quench was recorded. This always comes first. |
| **"as of" a date** next to a reading | That sensor hasn't reported in more than a day. |
| A greyed value with **‡** | The reading is physically impossible and was not used. |
| A small **ᶜ** | The value was worked out from other readings, not measured directly. |
| **Data issues** section | One or more systems have a monitoring problem rather than a magnet problem. |
| **Could not be analyzed** section | A system had no data for the period. |
| **Limited coverage** section | Systems from manufacturers we read environmental data for only (e.g. Hitachi, Canon, Toshiba). |
| **Excluded systems** note | Systems were deliberately left out. The note says which ones and why. |
| System Activity pages | Only for systems that need attention, have a data issue, or couldn't be analyzed. Healthy systems don't get one. |

## How to ask for a report

Tell your Avante contact:

1. **Which report** from the menu above.
2. **Which systems:** customer, sites, or specific MRIs.
3. **How far back** and **how often.**
4. **Who should receive it.**
5. Whether you'd like the **System Activity add-on**.
